Tic-Tac-Toe

Direct Manipulation + Menu Selection
Interaction design notes
  • Direct manipulation: tap a square and your mark appears instantly, with no typing or commands.
  • Menu selection: mode and difficulty are chosen from a short list, so players recognise options instead of recalling them.
  • Error recovery: an Undo button lets players take back a move.
  • Accessibility: large touch targets, keyboard play (Tab + Enter), colour plus symbol (X / O), and a live status message for screen readers.

Memory Match

Direct Manipulation + Touch
Interaction design notes
  • Direct manipulation / touch: cards are flipped by tapping them, with immediate visual feedback.
  • Visible state: open, matched and hidden cards look different, and the move counter updates live.
  • Error prevention: cards lock while a pair is being checked, so accidental extra taps are ignored.
  • Accessibility: every card is a real button with a spoken label; matched cards are announced, not just coloured green.

Word Scramble

Question-and-Answer Dialogue + Form Filling
Interaction design notes
  • Question-and-answer dialogue: the game asks one question at a time and responds to each answer, with a progress bar showing how far you are.
  • Form filling: a labelled input with validation next to the field. Wrong answers keep what you typed so you can fix it.
  • Plain-language feedback: messages like "Close! Try again" instead of error codes.
  • Recovery and control: Hint and Skip buttons let players get unstuck without being punished.
